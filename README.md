# Awesome Etsy + Pinterest [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of the best tools, resources and guides for driving **Pinterest traffic to your Etsy shop** — pin creation, SEO, scheduling, analytics and more.

Pinterest is a visual search engine that sends the largest share of social traffic to Etsy, and a single pin keeps working for months. This list collects the tools and resources Etsy sellers actually use to turn listings into evergreen Pinterest traffic.

## Pin Creation & Automation

Turn products into scroll-stopping pins (and, increasingly, do it on autopilot).

- [PlumeLark](https://plumelark.com) — Purpose-built for Etsy sellers: paste a listing and it designs Pinterest-ready pins, writes SEO titles/descriptions/keywords, and schedules them on autopilot. Includes a free pin/SEO toolkit.
- [BlogToPin](https://www.blogtopin.com) — Auto-creates pins from blog posts and shops, with daily hands-off generation and underperformer pruning.
- [Tailwind](https://www.tailwindapp.com) — Long-standing Pinterest (and Instagram) scheduler with SmartLoop and fresh-variant features.
- [Pin Generator](https://pin-generator.com) — Bulk AI pin creation with Etsy/Shopify import and Pinterest keyword research.
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com) — General-purpose design editor with Pinterest pin templates and bulk create.

## Free Tools (no sign-up)

Instant, browser-based generators you can use without an account.

- [PlumeLark Free Pinterest & Etsy Tools](https://plumelark.com/tools) — Hashtag, pin title, description, keyword, board-name and alt-text generators, plus Etsy tag & title generators and a pin-idea generator. Live character counters against Pinterest's limits.
- [Pinterest Trends](https://trends.pinterest.com) — Pinterest's own free trend explorer for seasonal and rising search terms.
- [Pinterest Predicts](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/predicts/) — Pinterest's annual trend forecast, useful for planning seasonal content.

## Keyword & Trend Research

## Pinterest SEO Tips

A few evergreen, source-backed fundamentals for Etsy sellers:

- **Front-load keywords.** Lead your pin **title** with the exact phrase a shopper would search; titles allow 100 characters but only ~the first 40 show in the feed.
- **Descriptions are for search.** They allow 500 characters, but Pinterest reads the first ~60 most. Top pins average ~220–250 characters — don't max it out.
- **Hashtags are minor in 2026.** Use 2–5 relevant ones; keyword-rich descriptions matter far more than tag count.
- **Fresh pins win.** Pinterest favors new content, so post multiple *angles* of the same product (gift guide, how-to, lifestyle, seasonal) rather than the same image repeatedly.
- **Alt text describes the image.** Aim for ~125 characters describing what's visually in the pin — no hashtags, no CTA.
- **Link straight to the listing.** Send each pin to the specific Etsy product URL, not your shop home.
